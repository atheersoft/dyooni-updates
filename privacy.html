Privacy Policy for Dyooni

Last Updated: 2026

Introduction

At AtheerSoft, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. Dyooni is an accounting application for managing accounts, debts, and financial transactions.

Data We Collect

  • Local Data: Account names, phone numbers, addresses, transaction records, and balances are stored locally on your device.
  • Contacts: Read only to import customer names and phone numbers. We do not modify or delete contacts.
  • Photos: For attachment photos and report logos.
  • Device ID: To verify app integrity and prevent illegal copying.

How We Use Data

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not upload your data to any server
  • We do not share your data with any third party
  • Your data remains private

Backup

You can create a backup of your database and save it to Google Drive. Backups are encrypted and protected.

Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect non-personal data for personalized ads.

Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your device
  • Android's built-in security protects your data from other apps
  • Your app password (if enabled) is encrypted using secure storage
  • Biometric authentication can be used to unlock the app if enabled
  • We verify app integrity to prevent tampering

App Permissions

  • Camera: For capturing attachments
  • Contacts: For importing customers
  • Photos: For selecting attachments
  • Notifications: For financial alerts
  • Internet: For ads and backup

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  • Open the app
  • Go to Settings
  • Choose "Delete Data" or uninstall the app from your device

All local data will be deleted immediately.

Children's Data

Our app is not directed to children under 13 years old.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at: atheersoft+dyooni.help@gmail.com
